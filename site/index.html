Storm Chaser Simulator

A Roblox simulator. You catch living weather spirits (sparks, gusts, hailstones, fog wisps, rainbow sprites) with a vacuum called the Storm Jar, take them home to your floating sky island and turn them into Charge. Charge buys upgrades, new sky zones and island decorations. You can hatch spirits into Stormlings, companion pets that multiply your Charge, and fuse three identical Stormlings into a stronger one.

What makes it different

  • Dynamic weather: a server-wide storm starts every 10 minutes on a fixed UTC schedule, adding storm spirits, boosting Charge and sometimes dropping Legendary pets.
  • Island building: players decorate and expand their own island on a grid, and some decorations give passive boosts.
  • Stormling fusion: four tiers (Base, Charged, Supercharged, Tempest) give duplicate pets real value.

Core loop

Catch spirits → the jar fills → go home in one tap → sell for Charge → buy upgrades and eggs → unlock the next zone → Ascend to reset for a permanent multiplier and Tokens.

Player retention systems

  • 7-day login streak ending in a guaranteed Rare-or-better egg
  • Session gifts at 5, 10, 15, 30 and 60 minutes
  • Pet index (collection log) with permanent bonuses
  • Global leaderboards
  • Safe two-sided trading
  • Offline earnings
  • Live events you add as a config entry, with no code changes

Fair monetization

Most Roblox players are under 13, so the game follows these rules:

  • Every egg shows each pet and its exact odds before you buy.
  • No fake timers and no "only N left" messages.
  • The shop never opens by itself.
  • Paid eggs never contain Secret pets.
  • Every zone can be reached for free. Passes only save time.
  • It follows Roblox PolicyService rules for paid random items.
  • Purchase receipts are deduplicated and only granted after a confirmed save.

How I build it

  • Design first: a full design doc covers the economy formulas, zone table, odds, monetization and art direction.
  • One config file: every balance value lives in a single Config.luau. Adding a zone, pet, egg, storm or event is a table entry, and cross-references are checked when the game starts.
  • Tested economy: 46 unit tests cover the economy math, save migrations, quest rotation, pass rewards and schedules. A pacing simulation fails the build if a balance change breaks progression targets (for example, zone 2 within 5 minutes and the first Ascension at 45 to 60 minutes).
  • Server authority: the server checks every catch, hatch and sell. Each remote has a payload schema and a rate limit.
  • Safe saving: a session-locked DataStore wrapper with auto-save, retries and versioned save migrations.
  • Mobile-first UI: big buttons, scaled layouts and nothing under the thumbstick or jump button.
  • Size: about 15,000 lines of Luau across 19 server services, 9 client controllers and 18 UI modules.
