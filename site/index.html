How I sell

  1. Build first. Before I ring a tradie, I've already built a demo website for their business.
  2. Call honestly. I say straight up that it's a cold call and ask for 20 seconds.
  3. Ask, don't pitch. How do their jobs come in now? Do they show up when someone Googles their trade in their suburb? What happens when someone rings while they're on the tools?
  4. Show the site. "I've already built one for you. It's done, and it's free to have a look at."
  5. Book a 10-minute look. The goal of the call is a short meeting at a set time, or at least the demo link sent and a callback booked. I don't quote prices on the first call.

The tradie sees their site before they pay anything. They deal with me directly: no call centre and no handoffs.

The websites

  • Built for each tradie's own business, trade and suburb, so they show up when locals search for that trade nearby.
  • Made to get calls and bookings, not just to look nice.
  • Mobile-first, because customers look tradies up on their phones.
  • Linked to their Google Business Profile and listing.
  • The tradie owns their customers. There are no per-lead fees, unlike Hipages or Oneflare.

Add-ons: text-message bots

Every add-on works by text (SMS) and is written in plain Aussie, so it reads like the tradie sent it between jobs. The tradie still closes the job.

The Tradie Lifeline (the main bundle)

  • Missed-call text-back: when a call rings out, the caller gets a text within about a minute asking what the job is and which suburb. The tradie gets an alert with the details.
  • After-hours answering: sorts emergencies from bookings at night and on weekends, and sends a morning summary.
  • Review requests: after a job, happy customers get a one-tap Google review link. Unhappy ones go privately to the tradie first.

Other add-ons

  • Quote follow-ups for quotes that went quiet
  • Invoice reminders with a pay link
  • Booking reminders that cut no-shows
  • Website chat that carries on by text
  • Quick quote and lead filtering
  • Messages to past customers to fill a quiet week

Live AI voice answering is offered last, to clients who ask for it, and is always clearly labelled.

Built on GoHighLevel (white-labelled), Twilio for numbers and SMS, and n8n for custom logic.
